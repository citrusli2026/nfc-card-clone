# Google Play listing (English)

## App name (max 30 chars)
Card Clone

## Short description (max 80 chars)
Read a card, write a blank, verify automatically. Three steps, no guesswork.

## Full description (max 4000 chars)

Card Clone splits "copy a card" into three steps, and shows you the result of each one.

**1. Read the baseline**
Tap the original card on the back of your phone. The app reads its UID, card type, sector count and every data block.

**2. Write the copy**
Tap a UID-writable blank card and write the same data to it.

**3. Compare automatically**
The app reads the new card back straight away and checks it byte by byte, block by block and sector key by sector key. You get a clear "Identical" or "Differences found", and if something differs it tells you which blocks and which sector keys.

**Why it is worth using**
- Fully offline: no network, no uploads, card data stays on your phone
- Verified results: not "written" but "read back and compared"
- History: every read and write is stored, viewable and deletable
- MIFARE Classic 1K / 4K, card type detected automatically

**Who it is for**
- Backing up your own access card so a lost card is not a problem
- Writing data to a blank card you bought
- Finding out what kind of card you actually have and whether it is encrypted

**About encrypted cards**
MIFARE Classic sectors need keys. The app ships a dictionary of common keys and opens what it can. Sectors it cannot open are reported honestly — it will not pretend to succeed. CPU and DESFire cards are not supported.

**Disclaimer**
Use this tool only for cards you are authorized to use. Copying someone else's access card or bypassing an access control system may be illegal and may violate local rules.

**Privacy**
No data is collected or uploaded. See the privacy policy.
