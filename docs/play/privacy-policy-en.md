# Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-07

Card Clone ("the app") respects your privacy. This policy explains what the app does with information.

## What we collect

**Nothing.**

- The app has no network access, no servers, and uploads nothing
- It bundles no analytics, ads, or crash-reporting SDKs
- It requests no permissions unrelated to its function

## Data on your device

Card data (UID, sector contents, keys) is stored only in the app's private directory:

`/sdcard/Android/data/dev.dsh.nfc/files/dumps/`

- Only this app can read or write that directory
- You can delete any record from the History screen
- Uninstalling the app removes this data
- The app has no export feature, so this data never leaves that directory

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `android.permission.NFC` | Read and write NFC cards — the core feature |
| `android.permission.VIBRATE` | Haptic feedback when an operation finishes |

## Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data.
